# FlockML Enterprise: Business Applications Matrix

## 1. Unified Infrastructure Layer for Multiple Enterprise Workloads

FlockML Enterprise is not a point solution or single chatbot. It is an on-premises artificial intelligence infrastructure layer designed to support multiple domain-specific intelligence workloads across an enterprise over time.

Once the private AI compute and retrieval foundation is deployed within an organization's network, the same underlying infrastructure can power multiple functional applications without incremental software license sprawl or redundant hardware investments.
